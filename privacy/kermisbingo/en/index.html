Privacy policy

This notice applies to Kermis Bingo (nl.kermisbingo.app) on Android and iOS. Controller: Johan C. Ordelman jr. V.O.F. (trading name Kermis Software), the Netherlands. Contact: johanordelman@gmail.com.

1. No account, local play

There is no account and we do not collect your name or email in the app. Drawing numbers and cards work offline. What you enter (draw, rounds, card series, display, voice, optional logo) stays in local storage on the device and is not sent to us.

2. Google Play / App Store — one-time purchase

The full version is product ID full_unlock (one-time, via the store). Google or Apple processes payment; we do not see payment details. The app only checks whether the purchase is active. Legal basis: performance of a contract. Retention: with Google/Apple per their policies; locally until you delete the app.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

In the free version you can start a short rewarded video yourself (no banners on launch unless described otherwise in the app). Google AdMob may process:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • IP address;
  • device type and characteristics;
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

Purpose: show an ad, measure it, and limit fraud/abuse.
Processor: Google (AdMob; on Android/iOS also the User Messaging Platform for consent; on iOS Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) may apply).
Legal basis: your consent (EU/UK) before personalised or measurable ad processing, via the in-app consent flow.
Withdraw consent: in the app via the privacy/ad settings screen (in Kermis Bingo: menu or settings → privacy & ads → change), and in your device settings for the advertising ID (Android: Google account → ads; iOS: Privacy → tracking / ads).
Retention: as set out in Google’s privacy policy and AdMob help; we do not store this ad data on our own servers.

Without consent or without starting the video, no ad data is processed for that video. We do not sell data.

3. Rights and complaints

For game data on your device, delete the app or clear storage. For ad and purchase data at Google or Apple, use their privacy tools. Questions: johanordelman@gmail.com. You may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority.

4. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children. Without your consent we do not serve personalised ads via AdMob.

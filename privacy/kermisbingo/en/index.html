Privacy policy

This policy applies to Kermis Bingo (nl.kermisbingo.app) on Android and iOS. Controller: Kermis Software (trade name of Johan C. Ordelman jr. V.O.F.).

Who we are

This app is provided by Kermis Software (trade name of Johan C. Ordelman jr. V.O.F.), Terbregsehof 48, 3062 CP Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number 98595407. Contact: kermisbingo@ordelman.net.

No account, local play

There is no account and the app does not ask for your name or email address. Drawing numbers, cards and settings work offline. What you enter (draw, rounds, card series, display, voice, optionally a logo) stays in local storage on your device and is not sent to us.

Purchases via Google Play and the App Store

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS): full_unlock (one-time full version). Google or Apple processes payment and billing; we never receive your name, address, email address or payment details. The app only asks the store whether a purchase is active and stores that locally on your device.

Processor: Google or Apple, for payment and purchase validation. Legal basis: performance of a contract (delivery of digital content). Retention: at Google or Apple under their policies (policies.google.com/privacy, apple.com/legal/privacy); in the app until you delete it or clear its app data.

Rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

In the free version you can choose to start a short rewarded video. There are no banners and no videos on launch. Google AdMob processes, among other things:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address;
  • device type and characteristics;
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

Purpose: showing and measuring an ad and limiting fraud or abuse.
Processor: Google (AdMob and the User Messaging Platform for consent; on iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency also applies).
Legal basis: your consent (EU/UK), which the app asks for before any personalised or measurable ad processing.
Retention: as set out in Google’s privacy policy; we do not store this ad data on our own servers.

Without consent, or if you do not start a video, no ad data is processed for that video. We do not sell data.

Deleting your data and your rights

  • Erase everything on your device: delete the app or clear its app data. We do not keep data on our own servers, so this removes everything.
  • Withdraw or change ad consent: in the app via Settings → Privacy-instellingen (privacy settings).
  • Reset your advertising ID: Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • How Google uses advertising data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, to the extent we process your data. Email kermisbingo@ordelman.net; we respond within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children. Without your consent we do not show personalised ads.

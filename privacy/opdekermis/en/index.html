Privacy policy

This notice applies to the fairground game Op de Kermis! (nl.kermissoftware.opdekermis) on Android and iOS. Controller: Johan C. Ordelman jr. V.O.F. (trading name Kermis Software), the Netherlands. Contact: johanordelman@gmail.com.

1. No account, local progress

There is no account. Game progress and virtual coins are stored only locally on the device; we do not receive that data on a server.

2. Purchases via Google Play / App Store

In-app products: full_unlock, munten_25, munten_75, munten_200. Payment via Google Play Billing or StoreKit; we do not see payment details. Coins are credited locally after purchase. Legal basis: performance of a contract. Retention: store data with Google/Apple; local until you delete the app.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

In the free version you can start a short rewarded video yourself (no banners on launch unless described otherwise in the app). Google AdMob may process:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • IP address;
  • device type and characteristics;
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

Purpose: show an ad, measure it, and limit fraud/abuse.
Processor: Google (AdMob; on Android/iOS also the User Messaging Platform for consent; on iOS Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) may apply).
Legal basis: your consent (EU/UK) before personalised or measurable ad processing, via the in-app consent flow.
Withdraw consent: in the app via the privacy/ad settings screen (in Op de Kermis!: menu or settings → privacy & ads → change), and in your device settings for the advertising ID (Android: Google account → ads; iOS: Privacy → tracking / ads).
Retention: as set out in Google’s privacy policy and AdMob help; we do not store this ad data on our own servers.

Without consent or without starting the video, no ad data is processed for that video. We do not sell data.

3. Link to kermis.nu

The app may open kermis.nu in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy; we do not operate it and do not know what data you provide there.

4. Rights and complaints

Clear local data by deleting the app. For AdMob and store purchases, use Google and Apple’s tools. Contact: johanordelman@gmail.com. Complaints: Dutch Data Protection Authority.

5. Children

The app is not specifically aimed at children. No personalised ads without consent.
